Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Telegram bot (“the Bot”) handles information provided by users while using its features.

1. Information We May Collect

The Bot may process information that Telegram makes available or that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Telegram user ID;
  • username, first name, and display name;
  • messages, commands, form answers, or other information you send to the Bot;
  • technical information necessary for the Bot to function correctly.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the Bot’s functionality, process user requests, maintain features, prevent abuse, and communicate with users when necessary.

3. Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold or rented to third parties. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the Bot, such as Telegram and hosting providers.

4. Data Storage

Some information may be stored for as long as necessary to provide the Bot’s functionality or maintain its operation. Data that is no longer required may be deleted.

5. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of the Bot by contacting the Bot administrator through Telegram.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection would be prohibited by applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot’s functionality changes. The latest version will always be available at this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Bot administrator through Telegram.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.
